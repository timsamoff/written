The Last Echo of the Siren

Fantasy Horror Zombie YA

The waves knew her name long before the sailors did. Along the jagged cliffs where salt-crusted granite met the fury of the northern tides, she sat watching the horizon dissolve into dusk. It was an endless loop of water and sky, a sequence she had memorized over three centuries of quiet isolation.

To those who traveled by wooden hulls and canvas sails, her kind were anomalies of malice—monsters hidden behind beautiful vocal frequencies designed to pull ships toward an early grave. But the truth was far less theatrical. It was born of deep, unmitigated boredom.
